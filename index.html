How this works
Under the TTE-like path, member contributions are paid from taxable income, employer contributions are taxed, and fund earnings are taxed during saving. Under the EET path, contributions and earnings are exempt during saving and tax is collected on withdrawal. Salary grows each year, so contributions also rise over time. Fractional years are modelled as a final partial year.
Member contributions are not reduced by income tax in the TTE path. The income tax is counted as tax paid, because contributions are paid out of taxable income. Contributions are treated as occurring evenly through each period, so they earn half of that period's return on average.
